# 🎬 FreeFrame Studio

**A free screen recorder and video editor for Windows: no ads, no watermark, no time limit, no sign-up.**

Record your screen, your face and your voice, edit it on a simple timeline, and save an **MP4** for YouTube or an **MP3** for audio. Everything runs on your own computer, and your videos are never uploaded anywhere.

> **Latest version: 1.4.2** · Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) · Free

## 🛠️ Install and run

### Installer version
1. Double-click **`FreeFrame Studio Setup 1.4.2.exe`**.
2. Choose where to install it (the default is fine), then click **Install**.
3. Open **FreeFrame Studio** from the desktop shortcut or the Start menu.

Installing it for your own user account doesn't need administrator rights.

### Portable version
1. Put **`FreeFrame-Studio-portable-1.4.2.exe`** anywhere: the Desktop, Downloads, or a USB stick.
2. Double-click it. The first start takes a few seconds while it unpacks.

### ⚠️ "Windows protected your PC"?
The app isn't code-signed yet (a certificate costs money), so Windows SmartScreen may show a blue warning the first time. This is normal for small free apps.

1. Click **More info**.
2. Click **Run anyway**.

You only need to do this once.

## ✨ Features

### 🎥 Recording
- Record your **whole screen**, **one window**, or **a selected area**.
- **Screen + my face**: your camera in a round bubble that you can drag anywhere while recording.
  - **Keeps your face centred**, tracked live while you record.
  - **Soft background blur** behind you.
- **Camera only** and **voice only** modes.
- Microphone and **computer sound**, mixed together.
- A **red border** shows exactly what is being recorded; the border itself isn't recorded.
- Floating controls stay on top of every app, with a 3-second countdown and pause / resume.
- **Hotkeys from any app:** `Ctrl+Shift+P` pauses, `Ctrl+Shift+S` stops.
- Choose 720p, 1080p, 1440p or native resolution, at 30 or 60 fps.

### 🎙️ Clean voice
- **Remove noise:** takes out fans, hum and keyboard clicks.
- **My voice only:** keeps the speaker closest to the mic and silences other people nearby.

### 🔍 Auto zoom and mouse effects
- **Zooms in automatically** where you click.
- **Cursor highlight** and **click ripples**.

### ✂️ Editing
- A timeline with **Video, Audio, Text and Overlay** tracks.
- **Trim**, **split**, and **cut out a part**: the gap closes on every track, so everything stays in sync.
- **Copy, paste, duplicate**, and unlimited **undo / redo**.
- **Play speed:** 0.5×, 1.1×, 1.2×, 1.3×, 1.5×, 2× or 4×. You can also speed up just one part.
- **Zoom effects**, crop, rounded or circle frames, borders and shadows.
- Volume, mute, and fade in / out for each clip.
- **6 colour themes** for the editor.

### 🤖 Smart tools
- **Auto captions** in 14 languages, including Hindi, with word-by-word highlighting. They export as `.srt` subtitles.
- **Remove pauses and "umm"s** in one click.
- **Transitions:** dissolve, fade to black, slide, wipe, zoom, blur.
- **Webcam background:** blur, remove, or replace it. No green screen is needed.
- **Colour filters** and brightness / contrast controls.
- A **blur / pixelate box** to hide passwords, emails or faces.

### 🔤 Titles, images and animation
- 8 text styles, plus shapes, arrows and highlight boxes.
- Images and logos, **stickers**, emojis, and **Subscribe / Like** badges.
- In / out animations, and looping motions such as bounce, pulse and float.
- **Templates:** intro, outro, chapter card, YouTube end screen, subscribe reminder, name tag.

### 🎵 Music
- **6 royalty-free music styles** created by the app itself, safe to use on YouTube.
- **Auto-ducking:** the music gets quieter while you talk.

### 📤 Export
- **MP4** (H.264 + AAC) for YouTube, or **MP3 / WAV** for audio.
- **Fast export**, about 3× faster than real time.
- **YouTube extras:**
  - Thumbnail maker
  - Chapters
  - Subtitles
  - **Make a Short** (9:16 vertical)
- Canvas sizes: 1080p, 720p, 1440p, 4K, Shorts / Reels, square and portrait.
- **Autosave**, plus **project files** you can open on another computer.

## 🚀 Make your first video in 5 steps

1. Click **Record** and choose **Screen + my face** (or just your screen).
2. Pick the screen or window, drag a box if you want only part of it, then press **Start**.
3. Press **Stop** when you're done. The recording lands on the timeline, with zooms on your clicks.
4. Trim the start and end, press **S** to split, or **X** to cut out a mistake. Add a title or music if you like.
5. Click **Save video → MP4**. Done! 🎉

## 🔒 Privacy

- **Your videos stay on your computer.** Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account, tracking or analytics.
- Face tracking, background blur, noise removal and captions all run **locally** on your PC.
- The only download the app ever makes: the first time you use **Auto captions**, it fetches the speech model once from Hugging Face (41 MB for *Quick*, or 77 MB for *Accurate*). After that, captions work offline.
- Your projects are autosaved in `%APPDATA%\FreeFrame Studio`.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Windows protected your PC" | Click **More info → Run anyway** (see [Install and run](#️-install-and-run)). |
| The antivirus blocks or quarantines it | This is a false alarm caused by the missing code signature. Allow the file in your antivirus, or use the other version (installer or portable). |
| The camera or microphone isn't found | Windows **Settings → Privacy & security → Camera** (and **Microphone**): turn on *Let desktop apps access…*. Close other apps that use the camera, such as Teams or Zoom. |
| Computer sound isn't recorded | Turn on **Computer sound** in the recorder before you press Start. |
| Other people's voices are still heard | Turn on **Clean up my voice → My voice only**, and sit closest to the mic. Use **Test** to hear the result in headphones. |
| Echo in voice-overs | Use headphones, so the mic doesn't pick up the playback. |
| Saving the video is slow | Close other heavy apps. Lower the resolution to 720p or the frame rate to 30 fps. |
| The app doesn't open a second time | It's already running. Look for it in the taskbar. |

## 🗑️ Uninstall

- **Installer version:** Windows **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → FreeFrame Studio → Uninstall**.
- **Portable version:** just delete the `.exe` file.
- To remove your saved projects as well, delete the folder `%APPDATA%\FreeFrame Studio`.
